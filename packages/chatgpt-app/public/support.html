Need help connecting or using the SkillWiki ChatGPT App?

Connecting to SkillWiki

To connect the ChatGPT App to your SkillWiki instance:

  • Ensure your operator has deployed the SkillWiki MCP server with OAuth enabled.
  • When prompted to authorize in ChatGPT, enter the vault's operator password.
  • SkillWiki does not use email links or third-party identity accounts. Authentication checks the password configured by the vault operator.
