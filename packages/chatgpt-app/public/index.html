SkillWiki

SkillWiki is an operator-hosted, project-aware knowledge base accessible via the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

ChatGPT Integration

When connecting the SkillWiki ChatGPT App, sign in using the operator password configured for your connected vault. Once connected, ChatGPT can search notes, read pages, capture thoughts and tasks, and publish typed knowledge updates directly into your vault.

Key Attributes

  • Operator-hosted: Self-hosted daemon with native systemd service and secure S3 synchronization.
  • Password-based OAuth: Direct authentication against your vault's operator password. No third-party accounts, Google sign-in, or email verification required.
  • Strict MCP boundary: Tools operate under explicit permission allowlists with compare-and-swap safety.
